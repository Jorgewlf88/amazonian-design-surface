# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are an international, cross-border community building an open archive of Amazonian surface design.
We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of nationality, language,
ethnicity, gender identity, age, disability, level of experience or artistic background.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:

- Communicate respectfully in English, Spanish or Portuguese — none of the three is the "real" language
  of this project. Do not penalise a contributor for imperfect fluency.
- Credit artists and translators explicitly.
- Treat Indigenous and local visual traditions as belonging to living peoples, not as free raw material.
  Follow [`docs/cultural-attribution.md`](docs/cultural-attribution.md).
- Give technical critique on the work — the repeat, the separation, the key structure — not on the person.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or dismissiveness toward contributors from any country of the basin.
- Misrepresenting authorship, including undisclosed use of unregulated commercial AI-generated imagery.
- Appropriating restricted or ceremonial imagery after being asked to stop.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, revoke contributor access, or remove contributions.

This Code of Conduct adapts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
