## What this is

`amazonian-design-surface` is a public, versioned library of original surface patterns inspired by the
flora, fauna and cultural heritage of the Amazon rainforest, across Ecuador, Peru, Brazil, Colombia,
Bolivia, Venezuela, Guyana, Suriname and French Guiana.

Every asset is production-ready: seamless, 300 DPI, colour-managed to CMYK Coated FOGRA39, and
traceable to the artist and the region that inspired it.

## What this is not

This archive contains **no garment manufacturing templates, no apparel cutting files and no tailoring
molds**. "Pattern" here means a repeating visual composition applied to a printed surface.

## How it is organised

The collection follows a three-tier textile surface hierarchy: **hero** designs carry the story,
**secondary** designs coordinate at a smaller scale, and **blender** designs provide structural texture
that reads as near-solid at a distance.
