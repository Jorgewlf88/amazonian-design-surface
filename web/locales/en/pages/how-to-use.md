## Licensing in one paragraph

Artwork is licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. You may print, sell printed goods and adapt the designs, provided
you credit the individual artist and this archive, and share adaptations under the same licence.

## How to credit the artist

Every design is the work of a named person who keeps their copyright. Crediting them is a **condition
of the licence**, not a courtesy: and crediting only `amazonian-design-surface` does not satisfy it.

Each asset card has a **Copy attribution** button. Use the short format on care labels, selvedge and
packaging:

```text
"Kené Geometry" © Ana Ruiz, amazonian-design-surface, CC BY-SA 4.0
```

And the full format on product pages, credits and licence sheets:

```text
"Kené Geometry" (amazonia-pe-hero-kene-geometry-v01) by Ana Ruiz.
From amazonian-design-surface: https://github.com/Jorgewlf88/amazonian-design-surface
Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: recoloured to a two-colour palette.
```

If you recoloured, rescaled, cropped or combined the design, say so. If a printed product genuinely
cannot carry text, put the credit on the product page, the invoice or an accompanying card.

Every artist in this archive is listed in **AUTHORS.md**.

## Before you print

1. Download the **TIFF master** from `dist/tiff-300dpi/`: it is CMYK, Coated FOGRA39, flattened.
2. Confirm the physical repeat size on the asset card matches your intended scale. Do not upscale.
3. If your mill uses a different ICC profile, re-separate from the tagged master rather than converting
   an untagged export.
4. Check the asset card for a **custodianship** note. Assets marked *review in progress* should not be
   used commercially until resolved.
