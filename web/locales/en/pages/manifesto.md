The Amazon is not a border. It is a single living system that nine countries happen to share.

We hold three commitments: **attribution over extraction** — every asset declares its regional origin
and its author; **production over decoration** — an asset that is not seamless, not 300 DPI and not
colour-managed is not finished; **the whole basin, in its own languages** — English, Spanish and
Portuguese are first-class here, not translations of an afterthought.
