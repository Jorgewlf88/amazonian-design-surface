Repeat checker

Drop your exported tile here to see it repeated. Everything runs in your browser: the file is never uploaded anywhere. Inspect the result at 25% zoom, where hairline seams and unintended diagonal trails become visible.

What to look for

  • A pale or dark hairline along a tile edge means a sub-pixel offset or a non-opaque background.
  • Diagonal or vertical trails mean motifs are aligning. Redistribute them rather than adding more.
  • If a full drop shows an obvious grid, an organic motif usually reads better as a half drop.
  • A tile that is not square still tiles, but declare the exact size in meta.json.
