## How to credit — required attribution format

Attribution is the one condition this archive will not bend on. Artists keep their copyright and
license their work here on the understanding that their name travels with it. Use one of the formats
below verbatim; each asset's `meta.json` carries every field you need, and the showcase site offers a
**Copy attribution** button on every asset card.

### Full format — documentation, product pages, credits, licence sheets

```text
"Kené Geometry" (amazonia-pe-hero-kene-geometry-v01) by Ana Ruiz.
From amazonian-design-surface — https://github.com/OWNER/amazonian-design-surface
Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes: recoloured to a two-colour palette.
```

Omit the `Changes:` line only when you have made none.

### Short format — care labels, selvedge, hangtags, packaging

```text
"Kené Geometry" © Ana Ruiz · amazonian-design-surface · CC BY-SA 4.0
```

### Inline format — a caption or a single line of body copy

```text
Surface design "Kené Geometry" by Ana Ruiz (amazonian-design-surface), CC BY-SA 4.0, adapted.
```

### Rules that apply to every format

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Name the person** | Use `author.display_name` from `meta.json` — the name the artist chose to sign with. A pseudonym is as binding as a legal name. Never replace it with the repository name alone. |
| **Name the asset** | Include the title and, where space allows, the full versioned identifier, so the exact design can be traced. |
| **Name the archive** | `amazonian-design-surface`, with a link where the medium allows one. |
| **Name the licence** | `CC BY-SA 4.0`, with a link where the medium allows one. |
| **Declare changes** | Recolouring, rescaling, cropping, combining with other artwork — all are changes and must be indicated. |
| **Proportionate placement** | Credit must be reasonably discoverable by whoever encounters the work. A credit buried where no buyer will ever look does not satisfy the licence. |
| **Multiple assets** | Credit each artist individually. A combined line is fine — `Surface designs by Ana Ruiz and João Pereira` — a collective "various artists" is not. |
| **Physical goods** | Where a printed product genuinely cannot carry text, place the credit on the product page, the invoice or the accompanying card. "It did not fit" is not an exemption. |

### What does not count as attribution

- Crediting only `amazonian-design-surface` and omitting the artist.
- "Design sourced from an open library" or any wording that erases the individual author.
- Attribution buried in a terms-of-service page unrelated to where the design appears.
- Removing or altering the `author` block in `meta.json` when redistributing the files.

## How this archive credits you

If you contribute artwork, you are credited:

- in your asset's `meta.json`, as the authoritative record;
- in [`AUTHORS.md`](AUTHORS.md), generated from that metadata;
- on your asset's card on the showcase site, in all three interface languages;
- in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) for the release that introduces your work.

You keep your copyright. You are granting a licence, not transferring ownership.
